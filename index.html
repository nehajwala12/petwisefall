The math

Each $1 of ads needs about $5 of sales to pay for itself. Our ads bring in $2–3.

The plan counted 40% of the shelf price as profit. But retailers keep part of the price, deduct fees, and the product has to be made. What is left for PetWise is 14–19¢ per retail dollar on Chewy and Amazon.

Walmart

Walmart is our best-paying channel, but we cannot see what we sell there

Walmart takes small fees, so Snooze keeps 48¢ of each retail dollar there, almost three times Chewy. The TikTok test should pay. The problem is measurement: our data only shows the few Walmart.com orders we ship ourselves.
