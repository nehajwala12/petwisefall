Attributed ROAS of 3.27x sits below the 4.55x breakeven once contribution is measured on PetWise net sales

The plan applied a 40% margin to retail sales. Amazon pays PetWise about 66% of its retail price and Chewy about 77%, before deductions. On invoice the full ask nets −$164K; after deductions, −$296K. Both are losses before any incrementality adjustment.

The August launches are selling in small volumes at Chewy, and the Meta planned behind them exceeds their sales

Chewy sold 55 bags of SmartyKat Treats ($549) and 20 bags of Chip Dips ($359) in August. Chewy’s own forecast for Treats has risen to 200 bags a month, about three times the actual pace. Chewy’s on-hand inventory of PetWise items rose from 133K to 275K units since June. Sell-through, not sell-in, now decides Q4 reorders, which makes in-stock items the right target for Chewy media.

Amazon’s own September results put the account at 1.99x before any of the increment is spent

From April to September, Amazon ad spend rose 74% to $266K a month while attributed sales fell 27%, as cost per click rose 152%. The recommended plan releases $253.3K now and holds $428K behind a holdout test

Tranche 1 funds what the launches cannot start without: creator content, reviews, Chewy search, Meta on established in-stock items and a Walmart TikTok test. Tranche 2 is the Amazon increment, which is mostly retargeting existing demand until proven otherwise, plus Meta on items not yet selling.

Creators create demand, paid social scales it, retail media captures it; only the capture stage can be measured today

The funnel logic is sound. The measurement is not yet: creator and review spend has no attributed return, and paid social is attributed by the retailer that sells the product.

Placement mix should shift from sponsored products to brand-building as items mature, on both Amazon and Chewy

The phase allocations are a sound operating rule. They describe how to spend within a tranche, not whether to release it.

Chewy Meta stays on proven items; Chip Dips, SmartyKat+ and Snooze wait for sell-through or stock

Meta on Chewy has returned $2.50 or better on established items. The recommendation keeps that, removes Treats until stock recovers, and gates Chip Dips and the new lines until they sell.

A four-week Amazon holdout reads before Black Friday only if it starts by 12 October

Tranche 2 turns on one number: the incremental return on Amazon’s added spend. Black Friday falls on 27 November. A test that starts on 12 October reads on 9 November and leaves two weeks to scale before peak; each week of delay moves the read closer to the peak it is meant to inform. The agency that runs the media should execute the test, not grade it.
